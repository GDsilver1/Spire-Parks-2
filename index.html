Build & test

Place a station. Pick a model, then tap the terrain or use Place Station. The New button starts another coaster; the Coaster menu switches between rides.

Drag the track-end handles or tap one to type an exact value. Length changes reach, Curve turns the piece, Slope raises or lowers it, and Bank rotates the rails. Tab to a handle and use arrow keys for fine changes. F brings the editor back into view.

Build below ground. Tunnels form automatically. Open Tools to paint entire tracks or individual pieces, place scenery, or delete a ride.

Keep your speed. Gravity moves the train. Pick chain lift for climbs, boosters for launches, drive tires for slow sections, or brakes to slow it. Test telemetry shows G-forces and an estimated sickness score.

Camera: Drag to pan, right-drag or two-finger drag to orbit, and scroll or pinch to zoom. Tests start outside; tap POV or press T to ride. WASD moves the camera, Enter builds, and Ctrl+Z undoes.
